### Forte Circuit: Ebb and Flow

- Lunar Cycle is activated by Closing Refrain or Resonance Liberation. It lasts 15 seconds, begins in Half Moon by default, and can switch between Half Moon and New Moon through Heavy Attack - Flux. ([Game8](https://game8.co/games/Wuthering-Waves/archives/524889))
- While in Lunar Cycle, Jump is replaced by Heavy Attack - Flux, Stamina does not recover while Iuno is airborne, and movement becomes Meandering. Meandering continuously consumes Stamina while airborne. ([Game8](https://game8.co/games/Wuthering-Waves/archives/524889))
- Environmental interactions, Utilities, or holding Jump ends Lunar Cycle. ([Game8](https://game8.co/games/Wuthering-Waves/archives/524889))
- In Half Moon, Iuno attacks with Moonring and restores Sentience when attacks hit. Heavy Attack - Flux: Moonbow deals Aero DMG, counts as Resonance Liberation DMG, and changes the state to New Moon. It can be cast in mid-air. ([Game8](https://game8.co/games/Wuthering-Waves/archives/524889))
- In New Moon, Iuno attacks with Moonbow. Moonbow Basic Attacks, Moonbow Dodge Counter, and Arc Beyond the Edge consume Sentience to increase their damage multipliers, restore additional Concerto Energy, and heal nearby team Resonators. ([Game8](https://game8.co/games/Wuthering-Waves/archives/524889))
- In New Moon, Heavy Attack - Flux: Moonring deals Aero DMG, counts as Resonance Liberation DMG, and changes the state to Half Moon. It can be cast in mid-air. ([Game8](https://game8.co/games/Wuthering-Waves/archives/524889))
- When Concerto Energy is full, Heavy Attack becomes Absolute Fullness. It ends Lunar Cycle, heals nearby team Resonators, deals nearby Aero DMG considered Resonance Liberation DMG, and creates a Full Moon Domain at Iuno's location. It can be cast in mid-air and can trigger once every 25 seconds. ([Game8](https://game8.co/games/Wuthering-Waves/archives/524889))
- Full Moon Domain lasts 30 seconds. Resonators inside periodically recover HP and Stamina. The domain's healing and Stamina recovery occur at the listed 5-second interval. ([Game8](https://game8.co/games/Wuthering-Waves/archives/524889))
- When a Resonator gains a Shield inside Full Moon Domain, they gain 1 stack of Blessing of the Wan Light; this trigger can occur once every 0.5 seconds. ([Game8](https://game8.co/games/Wuthering-Waves/archives/524889))
- Blessing of the Wan Light grants the receiving Resonator 4% all-DMG Amplification for 10 seconds, stacking up to 10 times. Gaining a stack refreshes the duration, and the effect ends early if that Resonator leaves the field. ([Game8](https://game8.co/games/Wuthering-Waves/archives/524889))
- Sentience has a maximum of 100 points. Intro Skill restores 40, Resonance Liberation restores 60, and Closing Refrain or Unfinished Refrain restores 25. ([Game8](https://game8.co/games/Wuthering-Waves/archives/524889))
- Moonring Basic Attack, Moonring Dodge Counter, and Mid-air Attack restore Sentience on hit while Iuno is in Lunar Cycle. The amount is not provided in the evidence. ([Game8](https://game8.co/games/Wuthering-Waves/archives/524889))
- Moonbow Basic Attack and Moonbow Dodge Counter consume Sentience in New Moon to enhance damage, grant extra Concerto Energy, and heal nearby team Resonators. The exact Sentience cost is not provided. ([Game8](https://game8.co/games/Wuthering-Waves/archives/524889))
- Arc Beyond the Edge consumes Sentience in New Moon to enhance damage, grant extra Concerto Energy, and heal nearby team Resonators. The exact Sentience cost is not provided. ([Game8](https://game8.co/games/Wuthering-Waves/archives/524889))
